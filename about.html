From a Small Studio to Your Pocket

Lumipuchi began in 2019 as a creative experiment in a tiny home studio. Our founder, inspired by late-night stargazing sessions, wondered: "What if we could carry a piece of the cosmos wherever we go?"

That curiosity sparked the creation of our first keychain - a hand-painted nebula encased in crystal-clear resin. Friends and family fell in love, and word spread quickly. What started as a hobby became a mission.

"Every keychain tells a story. Every owner becomes part of that story. That's the magic of Lumipuchi."

Today, with a team of skilled artisans, we've shipped over 50,000 keychains to customers across India and beyond. But our core belief remains unchanged: small accessories can make big impressions.
